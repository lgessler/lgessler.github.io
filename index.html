I am an assistant professor at Indiana University’s Department of Linguistics, with adjunct appointments in CS, CogSci, and MELC. Previously, I was a postdoc in the NALA Group at CU Boulder, and I got my Ph.D. in computational linguistics at Georgetown University with the Corpling lab and NERT. I am interested in low-resource NLP and language resource development, particularly in the context of endangered language documentation.
